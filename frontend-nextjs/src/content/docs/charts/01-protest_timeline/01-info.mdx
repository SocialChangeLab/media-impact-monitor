---
title: How to read this chart?
date: 2024-08-25
---

The **Protest Timeline Chart** shows when protests were held by the selected organisations within the specified time frame.
Each daily bubble summarizes protests with the same organising-group combination on that date. Wider views combine these summaries by week or month. Jointly organised protests are counted once in timeline totals.

## In the chart

- **Horizontal X-Axis:** Daily, weekly, or monthly timeline over which the protests occurred, depending on the size of the selected time frame.
- **Colour of Bubbles:** The colours correspond to the organisations participating in the protest (see the Legend below the chart)
- **Size of Bubbles:** The size represents the total estimated participation where a size is reported. Missing sizes remain unknown, rather than being counted as zero. Participants attending more than one protest may be counted more than once.

## Interaction

Hover over a bubble to see the organising groups, protest count and total reported size. Missing size estimates are indicated separately. Weekly and monthly bubbles can be clicked to narrow the date range.

Individual ACLED descriptions, locations, event types and event identifiers are not published. ACLED summaries do not link to individual protest detail pages.
