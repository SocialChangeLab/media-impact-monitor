<p className="lead">The Protest Timeline is the first thing you will see in the Media Impact Monitor dashboard, and it is the basis for all other analyses. 
It allows you to quickly get an overview of when protests happened, which organising groups took part, and their reported size. ACLED data is displayed as daily summaries rather than individual protest records.</p>
